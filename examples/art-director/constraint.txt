# Constraints, in the Oblique Strategies register. One per line, verbatim:
# this is a .txt bag, so commas and apostrophes need no quoting.
# Each line is a hard rule a reviewer can see you honoured or not. An
# adjective retunes a design; a constraint moves it.
No slide may carry more than twelve words
One slide is entirely black with a single line of type
Only one typeface, only two sizes
Every number is set larger than every headline
The most important slide has no words
Delete the second-best element on every slide
One slide is a single photograph
The transition IS the content
Remove every box, rule and divider
Two slides are identical except for one word
The first slide and the last slide are the same slide
Nothing is centered
Everything is centered
One colour, plus paper
Every slide must survive being printed in black and white
Cut the deck to half its slides, then cut two more
No slide explains the slide before it
One slide holds the room for a full minute
The client's name appears exactly once
Set the smallest type you dare, then go one step smaller
Every headline is a question
No slide has a headline at all
Show the number before you show the claim
One slide is a table and it is beautiful
Put the conclusion on slide two
The margin is the widest element on the page
Replace one slide with a photograph of a hand
Nothing moves, except once
The whole deck is left-aligned and stays that way
Three columns become two
The quietest slide follows the loudest
Type touches an edge on every slide
One slide is only a date
Say the hardest sentence first, in the largest type
Every slide passes the squint test at arm's length
Every image is full-bleed or absent
The agenda is deleted and never missed
One word is set twice as large as anything else in the deck
Colour appears on exactly three slides
Read it aloud; anything you skip comes off the slide
The deck is built in three dimensions: every slide is a view of one object
A full-bleed photograph on every slide, with the type set inside it
Type alone: no image, no chart drawn as a chart, no mark
Everything looks made by hand with a pen or a brush; only the captions are set in type
Motion is the content: every slide is a build of at least four beats
Not a deck but a document: each slide is a page from a printed report, photographed or scanned
